Sources & how to read this guide

This is a curated glossary of current AI development terminology, not an exhaustive taxonomy. Categories organize learning; concepts can overlap. Examples are illustrative. “Agent” and “foundation model” have context-dependent definitions. Each concept links to a course, paper, or official guide chosen for that term — not a single generic reading list.

Concepts reviewed September 2026. No live model calls are made by this guide. System walkthroughs describe example architectures. Progress is stored only in your browser.
